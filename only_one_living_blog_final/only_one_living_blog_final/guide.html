Beginner path

How to begin a low toxin life without feeling lost

This start here page gives you three simple focus areas. You can move through them in order or pick the one that feels most urgent in your own world.

Step one look around your main space

Pick the room you use the most. Clear flat surfaces of items that you do not need every day. Wipe them with a mild cleaner and open windows when it is safe and the weather allows.

Notice how the room feels after even a small reset. This shows you that you do not have to buy a long list of items to start living in a lighter space.

Step two notice what touches your skin daily

Take a quiet moment and gather the items you use on your body each day. Read the labels. Even if you do not understand every word you can begin to notice which ones feel long and heavy.

When one of these products runs out replace it with a version that lists short clear parts and a softer scent. Over time your shelf shifts toward a calmer mix.
